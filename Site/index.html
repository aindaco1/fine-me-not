One job. One brief siren.

Fine Me Not is an early beta for iOS 27. Enable warnings once, allow Always and Precise Location, and test the sound while parked. It checks approaching cameras on your phone, including with the screen locked. Warnings use your selected speaker, Bluetooth, or CarPlay audio route and media volume. Car audio still needs physical testing.

No app can promise uninterrupted warnings in every iPhone state. Power, permissions, interruptions, and iOS background decisions affect delivery. Reopen after a restart or force-quit. No ticket-prevention guarantee.

Quiet below the limit

The optional speed check is on by default. It skips speed-camera sirens when your measured speed is reliably below an approved posted limit or conservative estimate. School zones can use the lower reduced limit throughout the day. Unknown speeds, unresolved limits and expired evidence still warn; red-light warnings stay on. Road estimates are not field-verified sign readings. How it works and current coverage.

Privacy

Fine Me Not does not transmit your location, driving route, speed, or warning history. It needs no account and includes no analytics or advertising SDK. Camera matching happens on your iPhone. Settings, the offline database, and camera encounter IDs and times stay on the device; encounter state prevents repeated sirens. The latest audio attempt is also kept locally for troubleshooting, including the camera name, time, output route and playback result. No trip log is saved. You can choose to copy diagnostics; camera names may reveal a place you visited.

Database downloads contact finemenot.xyz over HTTPS, delivered through Cloudflare and hosted by GitHub Pages. These providers process connection information such as IP addresses and requested URLs and may keep request logs under their Cloudflare and GitHub privacy policies. No location is attached to the request. Optional TestFlight feedback and diagnostics are handled by Apple. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data; iPhone backups are controlled by your device settings.

Support & corrections

Report a bug or camera correction. Include the public camera/site name and a source if available. GitHub issues are public; do not post your route history or personal details.
